Sticker

Tinted cards with a coloured edge and a hard offset shadow, like stickers on a board. Zone labels handwritten.

Annotated

The clean style, plus a handwritten layer: zone labels in marker, and the focal box circled by hand. Precise drawing, human commentary.

Neon

Dark cards with a soft glow in each zone colour. The most Dracula of the four; keep it for diagrams with few boxes.

Sketch-lite

Chalkboard turned down: a faint hand-drawn wobble on filled cards, clean type. Reads as drawn, not as a draft.
