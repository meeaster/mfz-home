1 · Soft pastelRestrained

Sticker without the edges: borderless pastel cards, colour-tinted titles, zones as soft washes. The calmest option with colour.

2 · NotebookPlayful

Dot-grid paper, sticker cards, and every label the author adds (zones) in handwriting. The focal box gets a yellow highlighter swipe.

3 · Washi tapeCrafty

Paper cards pinned with a strip of coloured tape and set slightly askew. The focal card is taped in pink and sits straight.

4 · Layered paperBold

Each card is a stack of coloured paper offset behind it, and zones are big rounded colour fields. Depth without isometric drawing.

5 · ZineWild

Comic-book: solid Dracula colours, thick ink outlines, halftone dots in the zones, chunky arrows. Loud on purpose; a poster, not a reference diagram.
