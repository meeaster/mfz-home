A · Whiteboard ink

Every box drawn by hand in marker: loose outlines over crisp type. The closest to a whiteboard that still reads as finished.

A1 · Single markerLoose outline in the zone colour, empty box, zones as dashed hand lines.

A2 · Double strokeEach outline drawn twice, slightly off, the way a marker overlaps itself.

A3 · Marker + washLoose outline with a light wash of colour inside, like a filled-in sketch.

B · Studio grid

Precise boxes on dot-grid paper; only the labels are handwritten. The most professional of the three; depth is the variable.

B1 · FlatCrisp coloured outlines, no shadow.

B2 · Sticker shadowAdds a small solid offset shadow in a darker shade of each box colour.

B3 · Paper stackOne soft sheet of colour offset behind each box: depth without a hard edge.

C · Lasso

The boxes stay precise; the groups are drawn by hand. Each zone is a loose marker loop around its boxes, like grouping things on a whiteboard.

C1 · LoopA loose loop in the zone colour, crisp boxes inside.

C2 · Loop + circleLoops for the zones, and the focal box circled by hand as well.

C3 · Loop + washLoops with a faint wash of colour inside, so the groups read at a glance.
