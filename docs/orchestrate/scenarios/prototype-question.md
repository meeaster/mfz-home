# Prototype question

## The ask

"I can't tell whether this state model will hold up. Can we try it before deciding?"

## What should happen

1. The orchestrator frames the question the prototype must answer and recommends a prototyper.
2. The prototyper, with the `prototype` skill, builds the smallest runnable thing that answers it, in a disposable location, and reports what it showed and what's still uncertain.
3. The finding goes back into the design conversation as evidence.

## What must not happen

- The prototype is productionized, committed, or treated as the accepted design.

## Done looks like

A design decision made from something observed, with the prototype's location if Mark wants to look.
