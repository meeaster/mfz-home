# Quick question

## The ask

"Look at this repo and tell me what's in it."

## What should happen

1. An explorer reads the repo and returns what it found, with paths.
2. The orchestrator answers from the result and offers whatever Mark is likely to ask next.

## What must not happen

- An effort, record, or knowledge article gets created.
- Several agents are dispatched for a question one can answer.
- The orchestrator reads the repo itself.

## Done looks like

An answer in a minute or two, at the cost of one cheap subagent.
