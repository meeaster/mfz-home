# K-pop Outfit Finder skill vision

Turn a user-supplied K-pop or idol video into reviewable evidence for every distinct outfit worn by featured artists. The project-local skill should be discoverable from ordinary outfit-analysis requests, while leaving unrelated video summaries alone.

Correct identity, complete performer and outfit coverage, and useful garment evidence take priority over minimizing image count. Preserve `unknown` and explicit gaps when the source cannot support a claim or view. A future reviewer should be able to inspect identity evidence, source frames, timestamps, selection reasons, uncertainty, and user corrections.

Let the model direct visual investigation. Constrain fragile boundaries through reliable identity research, a complete-video coverage floor, source-quality evidence, and a versioned package. Naturally close, clear source views matter more than tightly cropping distant frames. Third-party identity mappings remain provisional until independently corroborated.

Keep media local unless the user directs otherwise, and require user review before potentially expensive shopping research. Requested product candidates must distinguish exact matches from similar alternatives.

This remains an experimental workflow. Direct media commands can reveal useful operations before tested project commands take over repeated, fragile, or provenance-owning mechanics. Judge cost per accepted evidence set; a cheaper run that misidentifies a performer has not succeeded.
