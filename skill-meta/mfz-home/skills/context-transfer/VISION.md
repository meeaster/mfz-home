# Vision

Context Transfer helps a different consumer use an artifact without reconstructing its source conversation. It serves direct cross-context requests and artifact-specific workflows when audience, access, privacy, portability, publication, or lossless handoff materially changes what must cross the boundary. Routine fresh-agent dispatch remains covered by the always-loaded agent contract.

Context fit matters more than maximum self-containment. Intentional local dependencies are valid when guaranteed by the destination; consumer type and reference accessibility are independent decisions. The primary consumer controls presentation, even when someone may later reuse the artifact with an agent. Consumer identity calibrates terminology, prerequisite context, detail, and tone; the user's request and intended use determine actions, questions, and artifact sections. Distillation can remove a source dependency, so references must earn their place through use, authority, verification, continuation, or revision.

Preserve the user's objective, rationale, priorities, and decision criteria without prescribing unsettled methods. An actionable specialist artifact remains complete and unchanged, with parent corrections separate and explicit. This lossless case does not require every ordinary artifact to retain all source detail.

Artifact-specific workflows retain format, storage, validation, drift checks, and authority, including remote draft writes when authorized. Their independence requirements are a minimum floor. Without an owner, drafting remains local unless the user separately authorizes publication. Unresolved privacy or disclosure conflicts require a stop rather than an incomplete or unsafe transfer.

The skill has no required platform, repository layout, private source, or publishing system. Success is a usable artifact and reachable context, without hidden assumptions, irrelevant production history, or a mandatory bibliography.
