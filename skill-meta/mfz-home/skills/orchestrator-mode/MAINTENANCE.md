## Model-following revision loop

Treat explicit instructions as probabilistic steering, not proof of adherence. For an observed miss, capture the tested skill revision, model, harness, and relevant configuration, then inspect the trace and artifacts rather than the model's self-report. Classify the miss as an absent rule, a weak trigger or competing hierarchy/salience problem, or ordinary execution variance. Prefer consolidating and repositioning an existing rule at the decision surface before adding text; remove distant duplication and avoid spreading one semantic rule across prompts, agents, or plugins. Rerun the motivating scenario and adjacent regression scenarios under a comparable test contract. Record limitations, and do not generalize one model, harness, or task into universal policy without broader evidence.
