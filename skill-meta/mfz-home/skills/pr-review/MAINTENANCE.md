# Maintenance

## Runtime dependencies

- `development-principles` owns the general design-quality lens.
- `thermo-nuclear-code-quality-review` owns strict structural maintainability and simplification.
- The caller supplies or identifies the PR, requirements or known gaps, repository instructions, architecture locators, validation evidence, accepted constraints, parent evidence, and uncertainty.
- The caller owns broad static, external, and current-state evidence gathering; the reviewer receives compact packets and retained session IDs when available.
- Model selection, permissions, dispatch authority, and publication remain outside the skill.

## Change procedure

1. Preserve the distinction between holistic due diligence and focused conformance review against an accepted design and known implementation history.
2. Keep supporting-lens detail in the owning skills; this skill composes their conclusions into merge due diligence and severity discipline.
3. Keep Blocker and Major findings evidence-grounded with locator, implicated requirement/invariant/boundary, consequence, and minimum remediation or evidence.
4. Treat missing evidence as reduced confidence or a complete bounded request, never proof of failure. Preserve every request field and keep returned packet conclusions subject to reviewer retesting.
5. Keep the reviewer's own tool use focused; broad corpus assembly and dependency research remain caller-owned.
6. Keep evidence requests, architect consultation, user decisions, mutation, expanded access, and publication authority distinct and parent-owned.
7. Run every scenario in `EVALS.md` after changing evidence requirements, severity, merge posture, output, or escalation behavior.

## Validation

1. Validate catalog and profile registration with Mindframe-Z.
2. Confirm the rendered skill matches source byte-for-byte and remains discoverable as `pr-review`.
3. Exercise complete-evidence, missing-evidence, severity, simplification, validation, escalation, and authority scenarios with captured artifacts when runtime behavior changes.
