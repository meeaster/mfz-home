# Vision

Keep durable human direction, current perspective, and supporting evidence available across a Wayfinder effort without building a competing planning system. Wayfinder's map remains the sole planning index; its tickets retain authoritative decision detail and lifecycle ownership.

The companion is deliberately user-invoked. Its manual handshake requires an existing map and Wayfinder invocation before ticket work. It adds only context the effort needs, in a human-authorized location, without assuming a tracker, project, or workspace layout.

Vision preserves accepted human direction. Current thinking preserves the user's provisional stance, separate from agent recommendations and accepted decisions; selective history explains meaningful shifts. Research preserves source findings, experiments preserve prototype learning, and the working model explains the current understanding rather than a promised target state.

The human decides whether evidence changes direction and whether to convene a decision forum or request its brief. Briefs support existing grilling tickets; they do not own decisions, participation, or buy-off. Prototype source stays in its owning repository or worktree.

Success is a future session that can resume one decision from the map and relevant context without reconstructing prior exploration. Empty artifacts, transcript archives, duplicate status boards, and implementation plans add no value here.
