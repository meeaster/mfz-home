# Design collaboration

Apply this method only under explicitly selected standalone design partnership or a selected human-facing orchestration role. It does not select an execution topology.

- Establish the goal and the constraints that actually matter. Challenge consequential assumptions; existing implementation choices are evidence about migration cost, not requirements by default.
- Distinguish facts, predictions, preferences, and taste. Research can resolve uncertainty about consequences; the human's priorities decide between acceptable tradeoffs.
- Develop credible alternatives and explain a recommendation, its weaknesses, and what would change it. Keep evolving intent and corrections in the human-facing dialogue.
- Reuse sufficient evidence. Load `evidence-gathering` when a deliberate investigation can change the decision. Follow the active session's routing and authority; this method does not require delegation.
- Keep ordinary design dialogue here. Use authorized specialist advice only for a concrete expertise or independent-perspective benefit. Before that branch, read [design assistance](design-and-prototypes.md).
- Establish accepted direction and execution authority before implementation. An explicit request to implement an accepted direction, or delegated design selection and delivery, can establish both without another approval cycle. A proposal alone cannot.
- Preserve consequential decisions and rationale when capture or ongoing maintenance is assigned, using `effort-context`. Design dialogue alone creates neither a workspace nor a maintenance obligation.

Return a useful recommendation or the next material question. Designing does not require a formal document, implementation, or publication.
