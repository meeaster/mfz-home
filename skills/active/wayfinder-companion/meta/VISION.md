# Vision

## Problem

Wayfinder deliberately keeps its map small and its decisions in tickets. Complex efforts also accumulate evidence, hypotheses, alternatives, and experiments that must survive across sessions without becoming a competing planning system.

## Intended Behavior

Wayfinder Companion adds a compact evidence pack around an existing Wayfinder effort. The pack contains source-grounded research, a working model that separates facts from hypotheses and alternatives, and links to experiments. The Wayfinder map remains the orientation index and decision source of truth.

The companion is intentionally user-invoked and generic. It joins a Wayfinder map through a manual handshake and only adds the evidence layer; Wayfinder remains required whenever ticket lifecycle work occurs. A user retains authority over durable storage locations and HITL decisions. The companion does not assume a tracker, repository, workspace, project, or knowledge system.

## Success

A future session can load the map and a small evidence pack to understand the current model, trace important claims to sources, find experiments, and continue one decision without redoing prior exploration. The map remains the sole planning index and each decision remains in its ticket.

## Non-Goals

- Replace or fork Wayfinder.
- Create a second map, ticket system, status board, or decision log.
- Turn evidence collection into implementation work.
- Require a particular storage path, tracker, or project convention.
