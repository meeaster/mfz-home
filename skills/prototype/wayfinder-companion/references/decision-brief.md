# Decision Brief Reference

A decision brief is a reader-ready supporting artifact for one user-designated Wayfinder decision discussion. It synthesizes relevant context so people can prepare before discussion. The linked Wayfinder ticket remains authoritative for the question, lifecycle, and final resolution.

Create a brief only when the user asks for a read-ahead, presentation, or in-depth shared discussion. Keep raw source evidence in research and experiment records; summarize the relevant conclusions here and link to them.

```markdown
# <Decision> Brief

## Question And Input Requested

<What feedback or shared understanding would help resolve the ticket.>

## Why Now

<What this decision unblocks and why it needs shared discussion.>

## Relevant Context

<The necessary vision principles, model constraints, and prior decisions, with links.>

## Options

### <Option A>

### <Option B>

## Comparison And Impacts

<Compare the options against agreed criteria. Cover relevant system, delivery,
operations, ownership, risk, cost, and migration effects.>

## Evidence

<Summarize the relevant research and experiment conclusions with links to the
source records and their uncertainty boundaries.>

## Recommendation

<Optional. Clearly label this as a recommendation, not an accepted decision.>

## Open Questions

<What the forum must clarify before the ticket can resolve.>

## Outcome

<After resolution, link to the authoritative ticket resolution and state only a
concise outcome.>
```

A Confluence page, slide deck, or other presentation can adapt this Markdown brief. Keep the brief as the durable source and link any presentation artifact from the ticket.
